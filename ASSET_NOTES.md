## Prompt de la imagen automatizacion

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Wide illustrative service image for Sinteria custom business systems. Editorial photograph of an organized contemporary warehouse workstation: close-up hands of a Latin American business owner using a laptop, tablet showing a sample inventory dashboard with barcode/product rows and blue-green graphs; boxes and shelving in the background. Natural skin detail, no identifiable faces, dark navy workspace with subtle cyan practical lighting and afternoon light. Emphasize connection between real inventory and digital control.
